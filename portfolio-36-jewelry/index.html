THE MAISON

goldsmiths.

Founded by Hélène Marchand in 1984, Lumière remains family-run. We buy gold only from certified recycled refineries and diamonds only with Kimberley certificates — beauty without compromise.

BESPOKE

From sketch to finger in four steps

I.

Consultation

A 45-minute private meeting — in salon or by video — to understand the occasion, style and budget.

II.

Sketch

Two hand-drawn proposals within a week, with stone options and honest pricing for each.

III.

Fitting

A wax model to try on. Adjust proportions, weight and stone size before anything is cast.

IV.

Reveal

Finished in 4–6 weeks, presented in our signature lacquered case with certificate.
