Savoir-Faire

Eleven Months. One Movement.

01

Design

Each reference begins as a gouache sketch in the Geneva atelier. Proportions are studied for six weeks before a single bridge is cut.

02

Machining

Plates and bridges are milled to 2 microns, then every edge is chamfered by hand — 4 hours of bevelling per bridge.

03

Finishing

Geneva stripes, perlage and black polish. A single tourbillon cage requires 3 days of mirror polishing.

04

Regulation

214 control points across 21 days in 5 positions. Only movements within −1/+2 s/day earn the Noir seal.
